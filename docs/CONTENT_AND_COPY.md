# Approved Content and Copy Direction

Use the following approved source content as the basis for the site. Copy may be edited for web readability without changing its meaning.

## Parent brand
**JH Studio Design & Development**

## Knitted Architecture
**Tagline:** Architecture that connects.

**Core idea:** Architecture should not stand apart from the people and places it serves. It should connect them.

JH Studio practices Knitted Architecture—an approach that intentionally weaves people, spaces, nature, culture, and movement into cohesive environments. Buildings are considered social and spatial ecosystems that encourage interaction, strengthen communities, and create meaningful connections between people and their surroundings.

## Vision
To design and develop places that strengthen communities, foster human connection, and bring people and nature together.

## Mission
Our mission is to knit people, place, and nature together through design and development. We create thoughtful buildings, communities, and environments that foster human connection, respond to their context, and create lasting value for the people and places they serve.

## Value proposition
At JH Studio, we transform ideas into places of lasting value. By combining design excellence, development intelligence, and our philosophy of Knitted Architecture, we help clients create buildings and communities that perform economically, connect people meaningfully, integrate with nature, and remain relevant for generations.

## Five principles
1. **Knit People** — create opportunities for human interaction and shared experience.
2. **Knit Spaces** — connect spaces through transitions, visual relationships, and shared circulation.
3. **Knit Architecture + Nature** — integrate nature from the beginning of the design process.
4. **Knit Context** — respond to culture, climate, movement, surrounding buildings, landscape, local materials, community patterns, and economic realities.
5. **Knit Movement & Function** — shape movement and relationships between functions to create connected environments.

## Writing style
- Confident, clear, human, thoughtful, and professional.
- Avoid inflated claims and generic phrases such as “world-class” unless substantiated.
- Explain design decisions in accessible language.
- Keep headings short and specific.
- Do not invent project facts, client names, testimonials, team bios, phone numbers, email addresses, awards, or completed work.
