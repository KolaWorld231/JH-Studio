# Team and Parent Footer

## Team approach
At launch, show only the current team members approved by the owner. Build the team section so future members can be added by updating data rather than redesigning the page.

Suggested fields:
- Name
- Role/title
- Short biography
- Portrait image
- Professional link(s), optional

All fields other than a name/role that the owner specifically provides should remain optional. Do not generate fake profiles.

## Parent-company footer
The shared footer should include:
- **About JH Studio** — parent company overview and guiding purpose.
- **Our Team** — current team members at launch.
- **Our Divisions** — Knitted Architecture and Real Estate Development.
- **Contact** — approved email, phone, location, or contact form.
- **Connect** — approved social links.
- Copyright/legal links if details are supplied.

## Parent vs division content
The footer describes JH Studio as a whole. The Knitted Architecture navigation and pages contain the detailed architecture philosophy, projects, design process, and studio-specific material. Avoid duplicating the full division content in the parent-company footer.
