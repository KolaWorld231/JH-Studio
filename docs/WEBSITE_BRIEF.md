# Website Brief

## Brand
**JH Studio Design & Development**

**Knitted Architecture tagline:** Architecture that connects.

## Purpose
Present JH Studio as a design and development company, then allow visitors to enter one of two distinct divisions. The launch experience should communicate architectural intelligence, care for context, human connection, and a thoughtful relationship between architecture and nature.

## Primary audiences
- Prospective architectural design clients
- Corporate and commercial clients
- Residential clients and development partners
- People exploring JH Studio's philosophy and work
- Future collaborators and team members

## Primary conversion goals
- Explore architectural projects
- Understand the Knitted Architecture philosophy
- Contact JH Studio about a project
- Learn about the parent company and its team
- See that Real Estate Development is an upcoming division

## Desired impression
Minimal, confident, professional, distinctive, thoughtful, calm, and contemporary. The site should feel like an architectural studio rather than a generic corporate template.

## Design concept
A restrained architectural editorial layout supported by fine technical linework. Architectural lines can draw in gradually, reveal a plan, and selectively transition into a building visualization. Motion should explain the design and reinforce the idea of connection, not act as decoration.

## Launch scope
- Parent-company landing page
- Knitted Architecture home
- Philosophy / principles page or section
- Project index with status filters
- Reusable project detail page
- Design process section/page
- Studio / current team section
- Contact page or contact section
- Real Estate Development Coming Soon page/experience
- Shared parent-company footer

## Future-ready requirements
The data model should allow more team members, projects, project categories, and Real Estate Development content to be added without restructuring the application.
